  2. GRINDING: how much does re-closing buy the firm?
     200 of 20,000 rows fraudulent, 200 rows sampled
     P(a single honest close escapes detection)  : 0.137 +/- 0.011
     closed form exp(-S|F|/N)                    : 0.135
     P(escape) after W =   1 re-closes            : 0.137
     P(escape) after W =   4 re-closes            : 0.444
     P(escape) after W =  16 re-closes            : 0.904
     P(escape) after W =  64 re-closes            : 1.000
     -> grinding is cheap. Unpredictability alone is NOT enough.

     The defence is that the anchor names the beacon round 1,000,000 it will use.
     Re-closing means signing a second anchor for the same period, and the
     first is already on deposit with the third party. Grinding is therefore
     not a cryptographic attack but a visible act of producing two closes.
     reusing the signature under a different round  : False  (must be False)
