3. does the generator change move the measured times?
==========================================================================
  Generators are inputs to the prover. Setup derives them and should
  get slower; prove and verify should not move. Measured at two sizes:

     n*m    deriv     setup(s)    prove(s)   verify(s)   size(B)
      64   legacy         0.03        0.51        0.31       688 ok
      64  RFC9380         0.13        0.60        0.38       688 ok
   1,024   legacy         0.51        9.65        5.12       952 ok
   1,024  RFC9380         1.93        8.55        4.96       952 ok

  n*m=64      setup x4.8   prove +17.4%   verify +22.7%   size identical
  n*m=1024    setup x3.8   prove -11.4%   verify -3.1%   size identical

  Proof sizes are identical, which is the check that does not depend
  on the machine. The prove and verify figures are single runs on a
  loaded desktop and differ by a few per cent in both directions; the
  reason to expect no real change is structural rather than statistical,
  since after BP.__init__ the two configurations execute the same
  operations on different points. Setup is about four times slower,
  and setup is not among the figures tab:bp reports.
